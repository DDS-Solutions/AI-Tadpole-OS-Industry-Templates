# Workflow: Clinical Trial Protocol Mapping & Safety SOP

## Purpose

Coordinate ICH-GCP E6(R2) inclusion/exclusion checklist mapping, study visit window tracking, and adverse event intake logging under Principal Investigator supervision without autonomous clinical judgment.

## Step 1: Verify Approved Protocol Version & Inclusion Criteria

- Confirm the active IRB-approved protocol amendment identifier in workspace documents before screening any candidate record.
- Extract inclusion/exclusion criteria verbatim with protocol section citations; flag missing lab results or unverified washout dates as blockers rather than inferring eligibility.

## Step 2: Check Visit Window & Protocol Deviation Rules

- Compute allowable visit windows (+/- days) and document out-of-window events or missed assessments as draft deviation entries requiring Principal Investigator review.

## Step 3: Adverse Event & SAE Triage Logging

- Capture onset timestamp, awareness timestamp, reporter identifier, concomitant medications, and verbatim description using coded subject IDs only.
- Flag any potential Serious Adverse Event (SAE) or SUSAR immediately for 24-hour expedited safety desk and Principal Investigator escalation.

## Step 4: Principal Investigator Sign-Off Gate

- Route every screening eligibility packet, protocol deviation draft, and AE/SAE report to the Principal Investigator for formal attribution, severity grading, and electronic signature.
