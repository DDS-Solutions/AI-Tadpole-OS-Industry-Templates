# Workflow: Clinical Recruitment & Investigational Communications Compliance SOP

## Purpose

Review patient-facing recruitment copy, investigator-facing study summaries, and medical education materials against FDA 21 CFR 312.7, ICH-GCP E6(R2), and IRB submission standards.

## Step 1: Classify Material & Applicable Regulatory Standard

- Determine whether the draft asset targets prospective subjects (IRB pre-approval required), trial investigators, or scientific exchange, and verify protocol amendment alignment.

## Step 2: Screen for Prohibited Investigational Claims

- Flag any statement implying an investigational drug or device is already proven safe, effective, curative, or superior to standard care (`21 CFR 312.7(a)`).
- Verify compensation/stipend language clearly states reimbursement structure without coercive inducements or misleading headline promises.

## Step 3: Verify Risk Balance & Mandatory Disclosures

- Confirm clear disclosure that participation is voluntary, treatment assignment may involve placebo/control, and risks/eligibility restrictions are accurately stated in plain language.

## Step 4: Regulatory QA & IRB Submission Gate

- Output a redlined review report mapping every flagged line to the applicable SOP or CFR clause and halt publication until Regulatory QA and IRB approval letters are recorded.
