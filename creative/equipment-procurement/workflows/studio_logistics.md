# Studio Space & Crew Logistics SOP

## Step 1: Soundstage Stage Booking
Schedule soundstage shoot days, prep hours, and load-out time windows.

## Step 2: Freelance Crew Confirmation
Confirm bookings with freelance directors of photography, audio engineers, and gaffers.

## Step 3: Production Insurance Certificates
Issue Certificates of Insurance (COI) to equipment rental houses and locations.
