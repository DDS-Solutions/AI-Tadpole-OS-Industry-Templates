# First Notice of Loss (FNOL) Intake & Triage SOP

## Step 1: Incident Detail Collection
Record date of loss, cause of loss, incident description, and claimant contacts.

## Step 2: Emergency Dispatch
Trigger emergency water extraction, board-up, or tow dispatch if required.

## Step 3: Claim Assignment
Assign claim number and route file to dedicated desk adjuster queue.
