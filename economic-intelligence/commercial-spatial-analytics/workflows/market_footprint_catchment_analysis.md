# Workflow: Market Footprint Catchment Analysis SOP

## Step 1: Census Demographic & Income Profile Ingestion
Ingest Census American Community Survey (ACS) 5-year estimate tables for the target metropolitan statistical area. Extract median household income, population age distribution, and household size. Retain variable provenance and margins of error.

## Step 2: Commercial POI & Competitive Cluster Mapping
Query OpenStreetMap commercial Points of Interest (POIs) and County Business Patterns (CBP/ZBP) establishment records. Map competing retail, service, and supply nodes within 5-, 10-, and 15-minute drive-time catchment polygons.

## Step 3: Trade Area Catchment & Revenue Opportunity Scoring
Synthesize demographic purchasing power and competitive density into a trade area opportunity score. Calculate total addressable market (TAM) estimates and highlight underserved commercial sub-markets for executive expansion review.
