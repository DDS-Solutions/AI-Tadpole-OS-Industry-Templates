# Workflow: Facility Hazard Exposure Quantification SOP

## Step 1: Tenant Facility Portfolio Geocoding & Value Ingestion
Ingest the tenant business portfolio of physical properties, leasehold improvements, and equipment replacement values. Geocode facility footprints and designate operational tiers (mission-critical, regional hub, satellite office).

## Step 2: Active Hazard Perimeter Intersection & Vulnerability Scoring
Intersect facility locations with real-time hazard layers (wildfire thermal perimeters, USGS shake intensity zones, coastal flood surge contours). Determine physical exposure risk and structural vulnerability scores.

## Step 3: Business Interruption Loss Matrix & Mitigation Directive
Calculate estimated direct property loss and daily business interruption impact against commercial insurance deductible thresholds. Formulate actionable business continuity directives including emergency personnel evacuation and IT failover activation.
