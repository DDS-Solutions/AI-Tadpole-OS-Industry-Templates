# Workflow: Supply Chain Disruption Costing SOP

## Step 1: Inbound Transit Delay & Telemetry Ingestion
Ingest live maritime AIS chokepoint queuing data, flight diversion notices, and severe weather highway closure telemetry. Correlate with active bills of lading and purchase order shipment tracking numbers.

## Step 2: Inventory Buffer Exhaustion Timeline Projection
Map delayed inbound freight quantities against distribution center inventory safety stock buffers. Project factory production downtime and store fulfillment stockout dates.

## Step 3: Daily Disruption Cost & Expedited Reroute Formulation
Compute estimated daily disruption costs (container demurrage, factory idle labor, contractual service level penalties) and formulate evaluated expedited freight bypass routes with associated incremental shipping costs.
