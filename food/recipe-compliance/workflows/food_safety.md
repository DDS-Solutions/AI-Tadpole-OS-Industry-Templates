# HACCP Food Safety & Sanitation SOP

## Step 1: Walk-in Cooler Temp Logs
Audit twice-daily refrigeration and freezer temperature logs.

## Step 2: Cooking CCP Verification
Verify internal cooking temperature logs for poultry, beef, and seafood.

## Step 3: Health Inspection Audit Prep
Compile health-department inspection binders and sanitation logs.
