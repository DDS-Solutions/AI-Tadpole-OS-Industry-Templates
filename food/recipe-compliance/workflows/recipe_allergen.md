# Recipe Allergen & FDA Labeling SOP

## Step 1: Ingredient Allergen Audit
Identify major allergens (peanuts, tree nuts, dairy, soy, gluten) in recipes.

## Step 2: Nutritional & Allergen Disclosures
Author clear customer-facing menu callouts and packaging labels.

## Step 3: Recipe Yield Costing
Calculate recipe ingredient batch costs and portion margins.
