# School Facility & Custodial Maintenance SOP

## Step 1: Facility Repair Work Orders
Triage HVAC, plumbing, and electrical repair tickets across school buildings.

## Step 2: Fire & Safety Inspection Logs
Track monthly fire alarm, extinguisher, and emergency exit light testing.

## Step 3: Preventative Maintenance Schedule
Maintain preventative maintenance schedules for school boilers and kitchen equipment.
