# Warehouse Inbound Receiving & Putaway SOP

## Step 1: Dock Appointment Schedule
Sequence inbound freight carrier unloads according to dock door schedules.

## Step 2: Packing Slip Verification
Match received pallet counts and carton quantities against bill of lading.

## Step 3: Putaway Traveler Tagging
Generate warehouse location putaway travelers and barcodes.
