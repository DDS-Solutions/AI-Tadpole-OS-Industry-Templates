# Inbound Pallet & Damage QA Inspection SOP

## Step 1: Freight Damage Documentation
Inspect incoming pallets for crushed cartons, water damage, or broken seals.

## Step 2: Over, Short & Damaged (OS&D) Report
Author formal OS&D damage reports with photographic evidence.

## Step 3: Carrier Claim Filing
Deliver OS&D documentation to logistics claims manager for freight claim filing.
