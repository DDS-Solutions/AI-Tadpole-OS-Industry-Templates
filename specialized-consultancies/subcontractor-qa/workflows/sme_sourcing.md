# Subject Matter Expert (SME) Sourcing SOP

## Step 1: Specialist Talent Identification
Source specialized 1099 consultants and technical SMEs for client engagements.

## Step 2: Rate Negotiation & Contract Audit
Verify subcontractor hourly rates against client engagement margin targets.

## Step 3: Subcontractor Onboarding Packet
Issue NDA, independent contractor agreements, and W-9 forms.
