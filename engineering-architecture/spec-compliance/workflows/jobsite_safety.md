# Jobsite Safety & OSHA JHA SOP

## Step 1: Job Hazard Analysis (JHA) Review
Verify daily trade JHA safety plans for excavation, fall protection, and hot work.

## Step 2: Toolbox Talk Logging
Record weekly jobsite safety meeting attendance and topics.

## Step 3: OSHA 300 Log Maintenance
Maintain project safety incident records and OSHA injury logs.
