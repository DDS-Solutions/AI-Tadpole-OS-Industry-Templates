# Patient Arrival Intake & Clinical Triage SOP

## Step 1: Chief Complaint Intake
Record duration of symptoms, appetite, hydration, and emergency red-flags.

## Step 2: Weight & Vitals Check-In
Log patient body condition score, pulse rate, temperature, and respiration.

## Step 3: Exam Room Allocation
Queue chart for attending Veterinarian (DVM) in clinical practice software.
