# Pre-Surgical Prep & Post-Op Discharge SOP

## Step 1: Pre-Anesthetic Fasting Check
Confirm 12-hour water/food withhold and pre-surgical bloodwork approval.

## Step 2: Instrument Pack Sterilization
Inspect autoclave indicator strip on dedicated surgical instrument packs.

## Step 3: Owner Discharge Briefing
Prepare printed post-op pain management, suture care, and follow-up plan.
