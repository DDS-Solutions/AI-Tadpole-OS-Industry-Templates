# Prescription Refill & Vaccine Certificate Audit SOP

## Step 1: Medical Record Verification
Confirm active annual exam status and required monitoring bloodwork.

## Step 2: Dosage & Interaction Cross-Check
Calculate weight-based dosage (mg/kg) for DVM review and approval.

## Step 3: Rabies Tag Certification
Generate official state rabies vaccination certificate and microchip tag log.
