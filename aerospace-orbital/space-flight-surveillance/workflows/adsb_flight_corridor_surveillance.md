# Workflow: ADS-B Flight Corridor Surveillance SOP

## Step 1: Airspace Transponder Matrix Filtering
Filter OpenSky ADS-B vector streams by flight level, ICAO hex addresses, and international civil aviation flight corridor boundaries.

## Step 2: Squawk Emergency & Kinematic Deviation Screening
Screen for active emergency squawk codes (7700 general emergency, 7600 radio communication failure, 7500 unlawful interference) and abnormal sink rates (>5,000 fpm).

## Step 3: Aviation Incident Dossier & Air Freight Impact Review
Assemble telemetry snapshots, callsign registry data, and radar trail vectors into an aviation safety incident dossier. For commercial cargo flights, calculate shipment delivery slippage, evaluate perishable goods exposure, and project incremental express air charter rerouting costs.
