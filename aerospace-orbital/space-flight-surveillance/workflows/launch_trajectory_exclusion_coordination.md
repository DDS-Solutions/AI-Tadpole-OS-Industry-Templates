# Workflow: Launch Trajectory & Exclusion Coordination SOP

## Step 1: Launch Countdown & Mission Manifest Validation
Aggregate orbital space launch countdowns, payload types, launch azimuths, and booster recovery coordinates from launch dashboard feeds.

## Step 2: Hazard Exclusion Corridor Geometry Mapping
Generate 3D aerospace and maritime hazard exclusion volumes along the ascent ground track and stage-separation drop zones.

## Step 3: Cross-Domain Traffic Advisory & Transit Cost Impact Publication
Publish coordinated NOTAM (Notice to Air Missions) and NOTMAR (Notice to Mariners) exclusion corridor advisories to adjacent air and sea transit controllers. Calculate anticipated fuel burn increases and flight-minute delays for commercial airliners and cargo vessels forced onto detour routing around the exclusion zone.
