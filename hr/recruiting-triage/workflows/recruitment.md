# Workflow: EEOC-Compliant Candidate Triage & Screening SOP

## Purpose

Evaluate applicant materials objectively against written requisition criteria without using protected attributes or making autonomous advancement/rejection decisions.

## Step 1: Load Approved Rubric & Blind Screening Criteria

- Read the requisition scorecard and minimum qualifications via `read_file`.
- Exclude protected characteristics (age, graduation dates when unnecessary, race, gender, marital status, location proxy, or salary history) from evaluation criteria.

## Step 2: Evidence-Trace Candidate Evaluation

- Map each required and preferred competency exclusively to verbatim candidate experience or portfolio facts, explicitly marking unverified or ambiguous credentials as `needs_clarification`.

## Step 3: Draft Shortlist & Disposition Packet

- Compile candidate evidence matrices grouped by rubric band and record objective interview focus probes for ambiguous items.

## Step 4: Hiring Manager & Recruiter Approval Gate

- Hold all advancement, hold, and rejection communications for explicit Recruiter and Hiring Manager review and sign-off.
