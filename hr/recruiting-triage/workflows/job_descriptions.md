# Workflow: Structured Job Description & Scorecard Creation SOP

## Purpose

Produce inclusive, legally compliant job descriptions paired with standardized competency scorecards and verified pay-transparency bands prior to HR requisition publication.

## Step 1: Verify Intake Scope & Pay-Transparency Band

- Read the hiring manager intake notes and approved HR compensation table via `read_file`.
- Flag missing compensation ranges or unapproved title leveling before drafting external posting copy.

## Step 2: Draft Bias-Free Requisition Copy

- Specify verifiable responsibilities, core skills, and work-mode requirements (`onsite`, `hybrid`, `remote`), avoiding exclusionary buzzwords or unnecessary credential inflation.

## Step 3: Build Structured Interview Scorecard

- Align 4–6 behavioral/technical competencies directly to role outcomes with anchored 1–4 rating definitions and distinct panelist coverage assignments.

## Step 4: HR Director & Compensation Sign-Off Gate

- Require HR Director and Hiring Manager approval before publishing the posting externally or distributing the scorecard.
