# Workflow: Interview Panel Loop & Accommodation Scheduling SOP

## Purpose

Assemble conflict-free interview loops mapped to scorecard focus areas, prioritize candidate ADA accommodations, and prepare calendar invites for recruiter release.

## Step 1: Check Accommodation Flags & Panel Assignments

- Review candidate availability, timezone, and any scheduling accommodation requests; route formal ADA workplace accommodations immediately to HR Lead review.
- Confirm each assigned panelist has a non-overlapping scorecard competency focus.

## Step 2: Propose Conflict-Free Loop Options

- Match available blocks from supplied interviewer schedules with brief interviewer transition buffers without inventing panelist openings.

## Step 3: Draft Candidate Confirmation & Prep Packet

- Prepare candidate-facing loop overview messages listing stage focus areas, video links/site arrival instructions placeholders, and contact points.

## Step 4: Recruiter & Panel Release Gate

- Hold calendar invites and external candidate emails until the Lead Recruiter approves the confirmed schedule.
