# Employee Offboarding & Access Deprovisioning SOP

## Step 1: Deprovisioning Trigger
Initiate departure checklist upon receipt of authorized separation notice.

## Step 2: Access Revocation Audit
Verify revocation of Google Workspace, Slack, VPN, and cloud credentials.

## Step 3: Hardware Return Logging
Log returned company laptops, security badges, and equipment assets.
