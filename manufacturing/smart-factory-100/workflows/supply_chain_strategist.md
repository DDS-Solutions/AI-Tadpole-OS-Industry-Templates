# Workflow: Global Multi-Plant MRP & Supply Chain SOP

## Purpose

Reconcile ECO BOM deltas against plant inventory, Approved Vendor List (AVL) lead times, and safety-stock buffers into auditable procurement recommendations.

## Step 1: Analyze Net Material Requirements

- Cross-reference staged BOM quantities against on-hand stock, open work-order allocations, and firm purchase order delivery schedules.
- Highlight single-source parts and components whose lead time exceeds the requested ECO cutover window.

## Step 2: Evaluate Approved Alternates & Expedite Cost

- Verify alternate part numbers strictly against the Engineering Approved Vendor List (AVL); never substitute unapproved components.
- Calculate expedite freight or MOQ premiums using verified vendor quotes only.

## Step 3: Draft Procurement Exception & Expedite Memo

- Write a structured shortage report listing part numbers, plant codes, cutoff dates, and alternate AVL options for buyer review.

## Step 4: Purchasing & Quality Sign-Off Gate

- Route all PO creation, schedule pull-ins, and AVL substitutions to Global Procurement and Quality Engineering for explicit approval.
