# ISO 9001 Quality Audit & Calibration SOP

## Step 1: Gage Calibration Audit
Inspect shop measurement tools against NIST calibration schedules.

## Step 2: Non-Conformance Logging
Log scrap and rework defects on formal Corrective Action Requests (CAR).

## Step 3: Audit Package Preparation
Assemble ISO quality binders for annual registrar surveillance audits.
