# Workflow: Accounts Receivable Aging & Collections SOP

## Purpose

Reconcile past-due customer invoices against unapplied cash receipts and prepare tiered collection drafts pending Controller release.

## Step 1: Reconcile Aging & Unapplied Remittances

- Read the current AR aging schedule and recent bank deposit logs to verify whether any overdue invoice has an unapplied ACH, check, or credit memo on file.

## Step 2: Triage Disputes & Escalation Tiers

- Separate active pricing/delivery disputes, partial short-pays, and >60-day delinquencies from routine Net-30 reminders.
- Never threaten legal action or propose bad-debt write-offs autonomously; route those accounts to the Controller.

## Step 3: Draft Tiered Collection Notices

- Generate polite reminder drafts citing exact invoice numbers, issue dates, PO references, and verified balances due.

## Step 4: Controller Release Gate

- Require Controller approval prior to dispatching any dunning notice or updating customer credit holds.
