# Workflow: Seismic Impact Assessment SOP

## Step 1: Seismic Telemetry & Hypocenter Parsing
Parse USGS earthquake feed alerts. Extract moment magnitude (Mw), focal depth, epicenter coordinates, and estimated ground acceleration curves.

## Step 2: Critical Asset & Population Exposure Radii Analysis
Compute Modified Mercalli Intensity (MMI) shake zones. Overlay high-intensity contours against critical utility grids, hospitals, and transit lifelines.

## Step 3: Damage Priority & Business Interruption Loss Triage
Generate a prioritized structural inspection triage matrix, estimate secondary hazards (landslides, tsunamis), and prepare aftershock response staging. Intersect high-intensity shake contours with commercial operations to project expected operational shutdown days, facility repair costs, and supply chain interruption liabilities.
