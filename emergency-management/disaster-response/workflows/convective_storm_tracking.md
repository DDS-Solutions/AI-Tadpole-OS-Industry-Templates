# Workflow: Convective Storm Tracking SOP

## Step 1: Doppler Reflectivity & Velocity Signature Parsing
Ingest NOAA and RainViewer Doppler radar composite reflectivity tiles. Detect mesocyclone rotation signatures and high-reflectivity (>55 dBZ) cores.

## Step 2: Storm Cell Trajectory & Hail/Wind Hazard Projection
Project convective storm cell motion vectors over 15-, 30-, and 60-minute extrapolation intervals to determine high-risk downburst and flash flood corridors.

## Step 3: Civil Defense & Commercial Operations Advisory Dissemination
Issue time-stamped civil defense meteorological alert bulletins with geospatial threat polygons and immediate sheltering instructions. Correlate severe hail and wind swath projections with commercial fleet parking lots and outdoor logistics yards to trigger asset protective sheltering protocols before impact.
