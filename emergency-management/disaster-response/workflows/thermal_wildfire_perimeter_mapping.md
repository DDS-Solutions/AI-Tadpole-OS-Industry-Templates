# Workflow: Thermal Wildfire Perimeter Mapping SOP

## Step 1: Radiometric Hotspot Cluster Ingestion
Ingest NASA FIRMS MODIS and VIIRS satellite radiometric hotspot detections. Filter by brightness temperature confidence thresholds (>80%).

## Step 2: Propagation Vector & Wind Alignment Modeling
Correlate thermal pixel clusters with ambient surface wind vectors and terrain slope angles to model active fire line propagation velocity.

## Step 3: Thermal Perimeter & Commercial Asset Exposure Delineation
Delineate dynamic wildfire containment perimeters, identify threatened civil infrastructure, and draft urgent evacuation buffer boundaries. Cross-reference tenant commercial facility locations and inventory warehouses within the 5-mile threat envelope to compute total insured asset exposure values.
