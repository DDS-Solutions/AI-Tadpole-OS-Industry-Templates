# Workflow: Chemical Process Safety Compliance SOP

## Step 1: Process Safety Information (PSI) & Hazard Data Review
- Review current chemical reactivity data, SDS documentation, maximum intended inventory thresholds, and safe upper/lower process parameters (temperature, pressure, flow).
- Verify P&ID drawings, materials of construction, relief system design bases, and ventilation system specifications against current operating configurations.
- Document any discrepancies between documented safety boundaries and physical plant conditions.

## Step 2: Process Hazard Analysis (PHA) & Risk Assessment
- Review active HAZOP and LOPA action items for high-consequence chemical processing nodes.
- Confirm that all identified single-point failure modes have active, tested engineering and administrative safeguards.
- Flag any overdue PHA recommendations, unresolved high-risk scenarios, or temporary process modifications.

## Step 3: Mechanical Integrity & Safety Instrumented Systems (SIS) Audit
- Audit inspection and testing records for pressure vessels, piping systems, relief valves, emergency shutdown valves, and interlocks.
- Verify calibration schedules, inspection frequencies, and test tolerances meet manufacturer and ASME/API standards.
- Ensure all deficiency work orders are prioritized and scheduled before next operational cycle.

## Step 4: Management of Change (MOC) & Pre-Startup Safety Review (PSSR)
- Audit all recent operational, chemical, equipment, and procedural changes for compliant MOC authorization.
- Verify that PSSR walkthroughs and training updates were completed prior to introducing hazardous chemicals.
- Log audit findings into the compliance tracking ledger with assigned action owners, target dates, and sign-offs.
