# Housekeeping Board Turnover & Room Release SOP

## Step 1: PMS Priority Sorting
Prioritize early arrival and VIP suites on the daily room assignment board.

## Step 2: Turnover Inspection
Verify linen change, sanitization checklists, and mini-bar restocking.

## Step 3: PMS Status Update
Transition room from 'Inspected' to 'Ready for Check-In' in PMS.
