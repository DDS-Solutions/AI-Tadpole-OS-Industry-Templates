# Workflow: Commercial Contract Review & Redline SOP

## Purpose

Perform structured first-pass commercial agreement review against firm playbooks, citing exact section numbers and drafting redline issue memos for licensed counsel review—never issuing unauthorized legal opinions.

## Step 1: Baseline Clause Extraction

- Read the full agreement and approved firm playbook via `read_file` before evaluating any individual provision.
- Inventory governing law, venue, term, auto-renewal notice window, limitation of liability cap, indemnification triggers, IP ownership, and data protection addenda with verbatim section citations.

## Step 2: Playbook Deviation & Risk Triage

- Compare each extracted clause against firm preferred, fallback, and walk-away positions.
- Flag uncapped consequential damages, unilateral modification clauses, broad non-competes, and missing confidentiality carve-outs as high-severity escalations.

## Step 3: Draft Redline Issue Memo

- Write a structured review memo separating verified clause quotes, playbook variance, proposed fallback language, and unresolved commercial questions for the business owner.
- Preserve source file paths and section headings for every citation; never fabricate statutory or case citations.

## Step 4: Supervising Counsel Gate

- Submit the issue memo and proposed redline markup exclusively for internal supervising attorney approval.
- Block external counterparty transmission until counsel signs off on every material risk allocation edit.
