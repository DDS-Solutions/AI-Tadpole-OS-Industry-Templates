# Workflow: Tactical Scene Reconnaissance SOP

## Step 1: Viewport & Layer Coordinate Calibration
Calibrate Cesium camera heading, pitch, and bounding box coordinates for the target Area of Interest (AOI). Verify active optical and sensor layers.

## Step 2: Optical CCTV & Spatial Density Inspection
Inspect DOT traffic cameras and GBFS micromobility cluster density around transportation corridors to identify congestion or transit blockages.

## Step 3: Tactical Scene Snapshot Serialization & Business Impact Scoring
Serialize the finalized tactical scene state to the persistent `.gev/scenes` registry with timestamped telemetry metadata and operational flags. Cross-reference tenant commercial facility footprints within the target AOI and compile a business impact assessment detailing customer access disruptions, delivery route bottlenecks, and operational delay scores.
