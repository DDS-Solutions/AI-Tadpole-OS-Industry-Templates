# Workflow: Overpass Infrastructure Validation SOP

## Step 1: Overpass Query Sanitization & Scope Bounding
Formulate and sanitize Overpass QL queries within bounded bounding-box limits to prevent denial-of-service or query injection against OpenStreetMap endpoints.

## Step 2: Critical Node & Way Topology Verification
Verify physical infrastructure nodes (bridges, tunnels, substations, hospitals, and communication towers) within the designated operational theater.

## Step 3: Infrastructure Vulnerability Matrix & Commercial Risk Formulation
Compile an infrastructure vulnerability matrix highlighting single-point-of-failure ingress/egress routes and co-located utilities. Evaluate commercial supply chain dependencies to compute estimated daily outage costs if primary arterial bridges or power substation nodes fail.
