# Workflow: Multi-Source OSINT Correlation SOP

## Step 1: Open-Source Broadcast Audio Ingestion
Monitor emergency radio broadcast channels and public safety audio streams for active dispatch calls, unit dispatches, and incident declarations.

## Step 2: Urban Movement & Spatial Telemetry Cross-Referencing
Cross-reference reported emergency incidents against real-time fleet, micromobility, and vessel traffic clusters to detect localized transit diversions.

## Step 3: Anomaly Assessment & Business Disruption Briefing
Synthesize multi-source anomalies into an operational intelligence briefing with confidence scorings and spatial bounding polygons. Correlate public safety incidents against tenant commercial assets to project local foot-traffic reductions, workforce commute impediments, and vendor delivery disruptions.
