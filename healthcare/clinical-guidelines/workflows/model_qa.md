# Clinical Protocol Evidence Validation SOP

## Step 1: Evidence Grade Verification
Cross-reference synthesized recommendations against Cochrane and PubMed indexed meta-analyses.

## Step 2: Dosage & Contraindication Cross-Check
Perform automated cross-checks against authoritative pharmacopeia indices.

## Step 3: Quality Assurance Signoff
Deliver final verified guideline package with full citation provenance.
