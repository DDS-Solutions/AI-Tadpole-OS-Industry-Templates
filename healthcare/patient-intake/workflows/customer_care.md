# Workflow: Front Desk Patient Care & Appointment Follow-Up SOP

## Purpose

Prepare respectful, minimum-necessary patient confirmation notices, pre-visit prep instructions, and billing/insurance intake follow-ups strictly within approved clinic templates and staff oversight.

## Step 1: Validate Approved Communication Channel

- Confirm the patient's recorded contact channel preference (patient portal, voice callback, SMS, or email) and verify Privacy Officer clearance before drafting any message.
- Exclude clinical advice, test interpretations, or unredacted sensitive PHI from SMS and standard email drafts.

## Step 2: Compose Appointment & Pre-Visit Prep Draft

- Include only date, time, clinic location, check-in window, photo ID/insurance card reminder, and approved clinic prep sheet references.
- Clearly mark provisional slots as pending confirmation until staff approves release.

## Step 3: Route Complaints, Cancellations, and Clinical Questions

- Immediately flag medication questions, symptom updates, billing disputes, and urgent cancellation requests for Front Desk Supervisor or clinical nurse follow-up.

## Step 4: Front Desk Sign-Off Gate

- Require human staff review and approval before sending every patient-facing message or recording a schedule change.
