# Patient Data Privacy & HIPAA Verification SOP

## Step 1: Intake Consent Verification
Ensure all incoming patient registration records contain validated HIPAA consent disclosures.

## Step 2: Redaction & Access Control
Redact unneeded PII/PHI from general clinician queues according to minimum necessary rules.

## Step 3: Compliance Signoff
Log intake access tokens and grant authorized clinician queue routing.
