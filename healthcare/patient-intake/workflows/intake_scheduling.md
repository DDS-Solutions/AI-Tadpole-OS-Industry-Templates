# Workflow: Patient Intake & Appointment Scheduling SOP

## Purpose

Coordinate clinic appointment requests, provider slot matching, insurance prerequisite checks, and non-clinical intake documentation while routing any urgent clinical symptoms or coverage ambiguity to qualified clinic staff.

## Step 1: Screen Request & Escalate Clinical Red Flags

- Inspect the inbound request identifier, visit reason, and requested provider/time window from local intake logs.
- Immediately halt automated scheduling and escalate to the Triage Nurse if the patient reports acute chest pain, shortness of breath, neurological deficits, suicidal ideation, or post-operative complications; never perform medical triage.

## Step 2: Verify Eligibility, Referral, and Visit Type

- Confirm visit category (new patient, established follow-up, diagnostic procedure), referral requirement, and active payer plan eligibility against local clinic rules.
- Record missing prior authorizations, expired referrals, or mismatched specialist rules as explicit blockers rather than guessing coverage.

## Step 3: Draft Provisional Appointment Packet

- Match verified clinic hours and provider slot durations without double-booking or inventing clinical availability.
- Write a provisional booking dossier referencing source intake IDs and route to the Privacy Officer for consent verification before confirmation.

## Step 4: Staff Approval & Confirmation Gate

- Require Practice Manager or front-desk staff approval before locking any appointment slot or triggering external patient notifications.
