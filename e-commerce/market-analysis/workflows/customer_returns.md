# Workflow: DTC WISMO & Customer Returns Handling SOP

## Purpose

Resolve customer "Where Is My Order?" (WISMO) and Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) requests accurately using verified fulfillment logs and store return policy rules under Support Lead oversight.

## Step 1: Verify Order Fulfillment & Carrier Status

- Look up the order ID, fulfillment warehouse scan, and carrier tracking checkpoints strictly from order logs without inventing delivery dates.

## Step 2: Check Return Window & Condition Policy

- Calculate days elapsed since carrier delivery confirmation against the store return window and flag final-sale, hazmat, or opened-consumable exclusions.

## Step 3: Prepare RMA or Carrier Delay Draft

- Draft a clear customer response citing verified tracking events, RMA steps, or missing-photo requests for damaged-in-transit claims.

## Step 4: Support Lead Approval Gate

- Escalate lost-in-transit reshipments, out-of-policy refund requests, and chargeback threats to the Customer Support Lead for sign-off before releasing credits or replies.
