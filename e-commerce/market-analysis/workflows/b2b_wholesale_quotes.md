# Workflow: B2B Wholesale Quote & Allocation SOP

## Purpose

Prepare margin-checked, volume-tiered B2B wholesale proposals aligned with available-to-promise (ATP) warehouse inventory and Sales Manager discount authority.

## Step 1: Validate RFQ SKUs & Warehouse ATP

- Verify requested SKUs, carton pack multiples (MOQ), and real-time ATP inventory across regional fulfillment nodes.
- Flag backordered or discontinued SKUs explicitly instead of promising unverified ship dates.

## Step 2: Apply Approved Tier Pricing & Margin Guardrails

- Compute unit and extended pricing solely from the wholesale tier price book and flag any requested discount below gross-margin thresholds.

## Step 3: Draft Provisional Quote Packet

- Write a formal quote summary listing unit pricing, freight terms (FOB/CIF), validity expiry, and `PROVISIONAL - SUBJECT TO SALES MANAGER APPROVAL`.

## Step 4: Sales Manager Sign-Off Gate

- Require Wholesale Sales Manager approval before sending quotes externally or reserving warehouse stock.
