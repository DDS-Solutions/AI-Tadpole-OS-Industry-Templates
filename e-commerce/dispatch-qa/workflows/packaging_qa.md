# Shipping Package & Weight Verification SOP

## Step 1: Package Weight Audit
Audit actual package weights and dimensions against carrier billings to prevent chargebacks.

## Step 2: Barcode Label Verification
Verify GS1-128 and UPC barcodes are scannable by carrier sorters.

## Step 3: Hazardous Consumer Goods Rules
Ensure consumer lithium battery and ORM-D markings comply with carrier guidelines.
