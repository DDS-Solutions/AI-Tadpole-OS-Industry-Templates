# DOT Hours of Service & Inspection SOP

## Step 1: ELD Log Audit
Audit driver duty status records against 11-hour driving and 14-hour duty limits.

## Step 2: Annual DOT Inspection Records
Track periodic DOT vehicle inspection certifications and cab decals.

## Step 3: FMCSA Audit Package Prep
Compile safety measurement system (SMS) compliance records for audits.
