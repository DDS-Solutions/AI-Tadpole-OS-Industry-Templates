# Confined Space & Trenching Safety SOP

## Step 1: Confined Space Entry Permits
Audit atmospheric testing logs (oxygen, LEL, H2S) prior to vault entry.

## Step 2: Trenching & Shoring Verification
Verify trench box and sloping protection meets OSHA excavation standards.

## Step 3: Safety Audit Dossier
Compile annual worker safety compliance logs for utility safety board.
