# High-Voltage Electrical Safety & OSHA SOP

## Step 1: Lockout/Tagout (LOTO) Verification
Audit electrical lockout/tagout procedures prior to maintenance energization.

## Step 2: Safety PPE & Bucket Truck Logs
Track dielectric glove testing dates, bucket truck inspections, and hot stick certs.

## Step 3: Tailboard Safety Briefings
Log daily crew tailboard safety briefings and job hazard analyses.
