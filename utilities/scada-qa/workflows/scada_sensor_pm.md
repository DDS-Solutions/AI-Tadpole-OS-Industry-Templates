# SCADA Sensor & RTU Calibration SOP

## Step 1: RTU Calibration Schedule
Track calibration schedules for remote terminal units (RTU) and pressure transducers.

## Step 2: Sensor Drift Log
Log anomalous sensor telemetry drift and schedule technician field calibration.

## Step 3: Instrument Replacement Orders
Draft replacement orders for obsolete PLC and telemetry hardware.
