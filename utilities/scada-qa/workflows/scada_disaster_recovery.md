# SCADA Backup & Failover Verification SOP

## Step 1: PLC Configuration Backups
Verify automated immutable backups of all operational PLC and RTU configurations.

## Step 2: Redundant Control Failover Drills
Log semi-annual hot-standby SCADA server failover test outcomes.

## Step 3: Disaster Recovery Runbook
Maintain updated emergency manual operating procedures for loss-of-SCADA events.
