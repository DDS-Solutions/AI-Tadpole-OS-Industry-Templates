# NERC CIP & SCADA Security Compliance SOP

## Step 1: Electronic Security Perimeter Audit
Verify firewall rules isolating SCADA control networks from corporate LAN.

## Step 2: Privileged Access Reviews
Audit quarterly operator access logs to industrial control systems (ICS).

## Step 3: Compliance Evidence Package
Assemble NERC CIP audit evidence binders for regional reliability entities.
