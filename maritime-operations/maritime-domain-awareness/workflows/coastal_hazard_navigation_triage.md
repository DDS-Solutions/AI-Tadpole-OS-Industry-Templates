# Workflow: Coastal Hazard Navigation Triage SOP

## Step 1: Navigational Hazard & Hydrographic Warning Ingestion
Ingest Notice to Mariners, reported shoal formations, drifting containers, and deactivated navigation aids along coastal fairways.

## Step 2: Drift Pattern & Search Datum Estimation
Calculate tidal current offsets and surface wind drifts to establish search datums for distress beacons and missing maritime vessels.

## Step 3: Maritime Safety Broadcast & Port Throughput Assessment
Draft standardized Coastal Warning messages for transmission across VHF maritime channels and NAVTEX automated telegraphy networks. Cross-reference navigational fairway closures with regional port terminal schedules to project cargo unloading turnaround lag and multimodal trucking transfer delays.
