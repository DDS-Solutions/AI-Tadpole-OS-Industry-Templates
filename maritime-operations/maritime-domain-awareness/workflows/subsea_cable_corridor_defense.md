# Workflow: Subsea Cable Corridor Defense SOP

## Step 1: Cable Route Spatial Buffer & Exclusion Zone Modeling
Generate protective buffer corridors along TeleGeography subsea fiber optic routes and terrestrial cable landing stations.

## Step 2: Vessel Loitering & Slow-Speed Drag Verification
Filter vessel traffic within the protective buffer for sustained low-speed patterns (<3 knots) or static anchoring indications indicative of anchor dragging.

## Step 3: Infrastructure Alert Dispatch & Economic Impact Escalation
Generate priority alerts specifying the offending vessel MMSI, flag state, proximity to subsea repeaters, and dispatch naval patrol intercepts. Assess downstream financial transaction latency risks and enterprise cloud telecommunications SLA breach liabilities in the event of fiber severance.
