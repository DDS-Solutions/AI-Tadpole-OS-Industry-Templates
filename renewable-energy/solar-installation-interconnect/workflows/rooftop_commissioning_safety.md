# Rooftop Safety & System Commissioning SOP

## Step 1: Fall Protection & Anchor Inspection
Verify certified harness tie-off points and ladder standoff brackets.

## Step 2: DC Lockout/Tagout Verification
Test zero energy state before combiner box wire pulls and inverter landing.

## Step 3: Commissioning Binder Sign-Off
Log string open-circuit voltage (Voc) and short-circuit current (Isc).
