# Third-Party Vendor Risk Assessment SOP

## Step 1: Vendor Security Questionnaire Intake
Evaluate prospective software and service vendors using SIG/CAIQ questionnaires.

## Step 2: SOC 2 Report Review
Analyze vendor SOC 2 Type II reports and ISO 27001 certificates for critical exceptions.

## Step 3: Vendor Risk Signoff
Publish vendor security risk scorecards for procurement approval.
