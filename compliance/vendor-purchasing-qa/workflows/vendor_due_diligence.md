# Supplier Due Diligence & Contract Terms SOP

## Step 1: Supplier Onboarding Intake
Collect vendor insurance certificates (COI), W-9 forms, and banking verifications.

## Step 2: Contract Compliance Audit
Ensure supplier purchase orders incorporate mandatory indemnification and SLA terms.

## Step 3: Approved Vendor List Maintenance
Maintain corporate approved vendor list (AVL) and audit status.
