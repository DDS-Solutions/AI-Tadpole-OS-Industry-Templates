# Workflow: OSHA Site Safety Brief & JHA SOP

## Purpose

Compile shift-specific Job Hazard Analyses (JHAs), verify hot-work/confined-space/trenching permits, and escalate imminent 29 CFR 1926 safety exposures to the Superintendent before crew mobilization.

## Step 1: Audit Shift Scope & High-Hazard Permits

- Read planned daily trade activities, crane lift plans, trench depths, and hot-work or energized electrical permits via `read_file`.
- Escalate any missing fall protection plan (>6 ft), missing trench box certification (>5 ft), or expired gas-monitor calibration immediately for stop-work review.

## Step 2: Prepare Daily JHA & Toolbox Talk Brief

- Map each scheduled task to specific physical hazards, mandatory PPE, engineering controls, and muster points using verified site records.

## Step 3: Track Near-Miss & Corrective Action Closeout

- Cross-check open safety punch-list items with photographic/written abatement evidence before clearing a work zone.

## Step 4: Safety Director & Superintendent Sign-Off Gate

- Require Safety Director and Project Superintendent approval prior to releasing the daily JHA packet or clearing prior safety holds.
