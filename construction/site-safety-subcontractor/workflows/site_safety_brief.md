# Daily Jobsite Safety & Hazard Inspection SOP

## Step 1: Perimeter & PPE Check
Inspect site access gates, hardhat/vest compliance, and perimeter silt fencing.

## Step 2: High-Hazard Activity Isolation
Verify lockout/tagout (LOTO) protocols and trenching shoring clearances.

## Step 3: Toolbox Talk Logging
Deliver and log daily safety briefing attendance and issue signed hazard notes.
