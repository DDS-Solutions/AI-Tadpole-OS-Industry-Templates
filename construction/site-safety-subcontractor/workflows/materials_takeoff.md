# Workflow: Blueprint Materials Takeoff & Bid Prep SOP

## Purpose

Calculate auditable quantity takeoffs from current drawing revisions, document waste factor assumptions, and escalate architectural/structural drawing ambiguities as draft RFIs rather than guessing quantities.

## Step 1: Verify Current Drawing Revision & Addenda

- Confirm drawing revision timestamps and CSI division spec sections against the project addendum log before counting quantities.
- Log superseded sheets or conflicting structural/architectural dimensions as formal RFI candidates.

## Step 2: Compute Net Quantities & Documented Waste

- Derive linear, area, volume, and fixture counts directly from labeled schedules, separating net material count from trade-standard waste allowances.

## Step 3: Price Check Against Vendor Quotes

- Map verified unit counts against current supplier price quotes, flagging quote expiration dates and long-lead mill/equipment delivery intervals.

## Step 4: Chief Estimator Sign-Off Gate

- Mark the takeoff workbook `PROVISIONAL` and require Chief Estimator or Project Manager approval before releasing RFQs or material POs.
