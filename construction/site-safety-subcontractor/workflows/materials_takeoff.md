# Material Delivery & Takeoff Variance Audit SOP

## Step 1: Bill of Materials Cross-Check
Compare structural framing and concrete takeoff sheets with delivery manifests.

## Step 2: Staging & Waste Management
Audit material laydown zones and record damaged goods return documentation.

## Step 3: Job Cost Variance Alert
Flag lumber, rebar, or dry-mix price deviations exceeding 5% budget variance.
