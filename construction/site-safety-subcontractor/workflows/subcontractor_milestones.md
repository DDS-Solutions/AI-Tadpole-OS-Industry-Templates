# Workflow: Subcontractor COI, Milestone & Mobilization SOP

## Purpose

Coordinate trade lookahead schedules, Certificate of Insurance (COI) and safety-orientation verification, and municipal inspection holds under Project Superintendent oversight.

## Step 1: Verify Trade Compliance Prerequisites

- Check subcontractor compliance registers for current Workers Compensation and General Liability COIs (valid through mobilization window), OSHA-10/30 roster verification, and executed subcontract agreements.
- Block mobilization notice drafting for any subcontractor with an expired COI or open safety hold.

## Step 2: Reconcile Critical-Path Trade Sequencing

- Review two-week lookahead schedules for work-area congestion, cure-time windows, and required rough-in inspection holds prior to enclosure.

## Step 3: Draft Mobilization & Exception Schedule

- Produce a trade sequencing brief highlighting dependencies, missing lien waivers, and prerequisite inspection checkpoints.

## Step 4: Superintendent Dispatch Gate

- Require Project Superintendent approval before issuing Notice-to-Proceed messages or publishing revised trade dates.
